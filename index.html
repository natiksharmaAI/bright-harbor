About Bright Harbor

Dentistry that feels more like a coastal retreat than a clinic.

Founded in 2011 by Dr. Maya Lindqvist, Bright Harbor Dental was born from a singular conviction: going to the dentist shouldn't feel clinical, cold, or rushed. Dr. Maya envisioned a tranquil sanctuary overlooking the harbor where patients look forward to their visits.

We remain proudly independent and staunchly dedicated to unhurried 45-to-60 minute appointments, 100% transparent pricing before any procedure begins, and a gentle team that remembers your name and comfort preferences.
